NovelBlush

Translated web novels, a new chapter every day

Stories that make you Blush~

NovelBlush is a mobile reading app for translated web novels. Readers follow long-running serialised stories — romance, Boys Love, Girls Love, historical, rebirth and transmigration — with new chapters added daily.

Download on the App Store

What the app does

  • A catalogue of over 37,000 novels, updated with new chapters every day
  • Recommendations based on the genres you choose
  • VIP offline reading — keep up to 100 chapters on your device
  • Dark mode, adjustable text and a distraction-free reader
  • Dedicated Boys Love and Girls Love collections
